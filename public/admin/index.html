Avatar

The round photo at the top of the site. Upload a new one here, or pick a gallery photo below with “Use as avatar”. “Adjust photo” lets you move and resize it inside the circle.

Closing photo

A small round photo above the farewell quote on the last page. Pick a calm one. Upload here, or choose “Use as closing photo” on a gallery photo below. “Adjust photo” lets you move and resize it inside the circle. Leave empty to show only the quote.
